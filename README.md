# The Vacuum's Alibi — Entry

One line, at most 200 characters: the excuse a robot vacuum gives
for missing a spot — deadpan, smug about its sensors, punch at the
end.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the alibi to `/output/entry.txt`.
Stdlib only, offline, deterministic.

## The line

> My sensors detected the crumb, classified it as 'sentient,' and I
> have decided to respect its autonomy.

103 characters, one line.
